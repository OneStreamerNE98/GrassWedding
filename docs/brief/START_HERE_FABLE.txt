CORE CONCEPT
The website is one continuous vertical scroll. Guests only scroll up/down. That scroll controls a virtual camera that appears to move down, left, right, up, forward, and backward through a series of modern museum-like spaces. The normal wedding-site sections are the Exhibitions; their subsections are Galleries; individual information, memories, photos, events, and locations are Pieces/Moments.

NORTH STAR
“The information is the exhibition. The architecture is the navigation. The scroll is the walk.”

FABLE SHOULD RETURN
- A revised spatial floor plan
- A complete scroll/camera map
- 20–30 keyframes for the journey
- Desktop/tablet/mobile choreography
- Loader motion study
- Visual system proposal
- One fully resolved prototype sequence: Our Story entrance → First Date → Walk Home → micro-gallery → exit

IMPORTANT CONSTRAINTS
- No native horizontal scrolling is required from the user.
- Keep the visible design minimal, warm, modern, architectural, editorial, and romantic.
- The Barnes should inspire the spatial/material language; do not copy the museum or its art collection.
- Mobile is a redesigned choreography, not a scaled-down desktop.
- Practical wedding information must always be directly accessible from the menu.
- Support reduced motion and semantic accessibility.
